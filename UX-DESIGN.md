| Log In | Sign Up | Home | Student Information |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| <img src="ux-design/Log%20In.png" width="200"> | <img src="ux-design/Sign%20Up.png" width="200"> | <img src="ux-design/Home.png" width="200"> | <img src="ux-design/Student%20Information.png" width="200"> |

**Log In:**  
Allows existing users to access their Violet Path accounts by entering their email address and password. It also includes a Forgot Password option for users who need to reset their password.

**Sign Up:**  
Allows new users to create an account by entering their email address, password, password confirmation, and current major. Users can submit the form to register.

**Home:**  
Serves as the main navigation dashboard of Violet Path. It provides access to Student Information, Courses, Degree Progress, GPA, Declaration, FAQ, and Department Contacts.

**Student Information:**  
Allows students to view and manage their personal and academic information, including their name, student ID, university ID, major, minor, academic year, and expected graduation date.

| Degree Progess | Requirements | Graduation Check |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| <img src="ux-design/Degree%20Progress.png" width="200"> | <img src="ux-design/Requirements.png" width="200"> | <img src="ux-design/Graduation%20Check.png" width="200"> |

**Degree Progress:**  
Provides an overview of a student's academic progress, displaying their current semester, major, minor, and completed credits. Students can navigate to the Requirements and Graduation Check pages for more detailed information.

**Requirements:**  
Displays the academic requirements students must complete to graduate. Requirements are organized into major, minor, and core curriculum sections, with relevant courses, credits, and grades. This helps students track completed coursework and identify remaining requirements.

**Graduation Check:**  
Allows students to monitor their progress toward graduation by comparing completed credits with remaining credits. It provides separate breakdowns for total degree credits, major credits, minor credits, and core curriculum credits.

|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| <img src="ux-design/Courses.png" width="200"> | <img src="ux-design/GPA.png" width="200"> | <img src="ux-design/Target%20GPA%20Calculator.png" width="200"> |

**Courses:**  
Allows students to search for available university courses. Each course listing provides information such as the school, instruction format (in-person or online), course description, and prerequisites, helping students make informed course selections.

**GPA:**  
Provides students with an overview of their academic performance, including cumulative GPA, major GPA, minor GPA, completed credits, and remaining credits. It also displays courses and letter grades organized by semester and category. Students can access the GPA Calculator to plan their future academic performance.

**Target GPA Calculator:**  
Allows students to enter their desired GPA and remaining credits to estimate the grades needed to reach their academic goals. It helps determine whether a target GPA is achievable and provides an estimate of the number of high grades required.

**FAQ:**  
Provides answers to common questions about courses, GPA, majors, and general academic concerns. Students can search for specific questions or browse categories. A Contact Support option is available for students who need additional assistance.

**Password Reset:**  
Allows users to update their account password by entering their current email address, a new password, and password confirmation. Users can submit the form to complete the password reset process.

**Declaration:**  
Helps students begin the process of declaring or changing their major. Students can search for a major and view a declaration form containing the date, reason, and student signature fields.

**Department Contacts:**  
Provides students with access to university department and academic advisor contact information. Students can search for departments, access department email addresses and websites, and find their advisor's contact information. The page also includes an option to add an advisor.
